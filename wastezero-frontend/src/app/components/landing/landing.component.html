👥 User Roles

Built for Everyone

Three distinct roles, each with a dedicated dashboard and workflow.

👤

Citizen

Schedule pickups, track status, view your recycling impact, and message your volunteer.

  • ✅ Schedule waste pickup
  • ✅ Track pickup status
  • ✅ View My Impact stats
  • ✅ Chat with volunteer
🙋

Volunteer

Browse open pickup requests, accept them, collect waste, and mark them as completed.

  • ✅ View open pickups
  • ✅ Accept & complete pickups
  • ✅ Apply to opportunities
  • ✅ Real-time messaging
🔧

Admin

Monitor the entire platform, manage users, generate reports, and control all activities.

  • ✅ Platform-wide dashboard
  • ✅ Manage users & volunteers
  • ✅ Generate reports (CSV/PDF)
  • ✅ Full activity logs
